## Warning scope and cause

118 SNR warnings affect 77 unique clean inputs: {'light_noise': 77, 'medium_noise': 35, 'heavy_noise': 6}. Species distribution: {'Chestnut-backed_Owlet': 2, 'Crimson-fronted_Barbet': 1, 'Green-billed_Coucal': 11, 'Spot-winged_Thrush': 3, 'Sri_Lanka_Frogmouth': 88, 'Sri_Lanka_Spurfowl': 9, 'Yellow-fronted_Barbet': 4}.
Source-content RMS for warning inputs ranges from -104.741 to -66.060 dBFS. 118 warning variants meet the -60 dBFS near-silence threshold. The threshold does not capture every quantization-sensitive example.
Each of the 118 warning mixtures was reconstructed entirely in memory using the original training clip, recorded noise file, resampled-noise offset, noise gain and common final gain. Re-encoding that reconstruction in memory as PCM-16 reproduced the saved waveform sample-for-sample in every case. Every reconstructed pre-PCM SNR matched its target; every recomputed post-PCM SNR matched the manifest. No wrong-noise, wrong-offset, wrong-parent, wrong-gain or clipped-mixture mismatch was found among the warning cases.
Every sample error was within one PCM-16 LSB. Intended noise is small relative to PCM resolution; PCM encoding introduces residual error, including bias, that changes the effective-noise SNR. Quantization-error RMS / intended-noise RMS ranges from 0.2471 to 36.7877. Thus these warnings are explained by low absolute mixture/noise energy and PCM quantization, not a source-selection or SNR-scaling error. They remain real saved-audio SNR deviations, not harmless reporting errors.
Maximum absolute deviation: 31.443752 dB. Effective SNR uses decoded saved audio minus the gain-scaled clean signal, so it includes quantization. An SNR warning does not prove that the target bird is audible or inaudible.

## Recommendations for a future training manifest

Preserve the current immutable manifest. Create a separate derived training-selection manifest only after approval, with fields such as include_in_primary_training, include_in_snr_controlled_experiment, review_required, selection_reason, snr_error_db and audibility_review_status. These recommendations have NOT been applied.
- Four digitally silent clean examples: retain in the packaged archive, but recommend exclusion from the primary species-classification training selection because they have no measurable source signal. No noisy variants exist for them.
- All 118 SNR-warning variants: retain for traceability; recommend withholding from the primary target-SNR-controlled selection until reviewed. Do not advertise them as 20/10/0 dB-compliant. Their 77 clean parents and other compliant variants are not automatically excluded. An explicitly documented non-SNR-controlled robustness experiment could retain them as noisy examples using measured SNR.
- Remaining 22,022 noisy variants: eligible on path/format/membership and reported SNR-tolerance grounds, subject to inherited provenance, padding and audibility review; not automatically certified useful bird examples.
- Low-energy/near-silent clips: review rather than exclude solely on RMS. The training quality table flags 193 near-silent clean clips. Keep measured energy and audibility status separately; the threshold alone is not a bird-presence detector.
- Heavy padding: 365 training clips have at least 50% padding. Retain and flag both parents and derivatives. Carry padding_fraction and source-content duration; decide a documented training policy after reviewing call content rather than dropping them automatically.
- All clips: audibility remains human_review_required. Preserve parent IDs, final groups, species and clean/noisy relationships. Unknown original-recording provenance remains unresolved, and six single-source noise categories cannot support a claim of independent-noise test evaluation.
If only the recommended four silent clean examples and 118 SNR-warning variants were filtered in a future approved primary-selection manifest, it would contain 7,380 clean + 22,022 noisy = 29,402 examples, before any audibility/padding decisions.
